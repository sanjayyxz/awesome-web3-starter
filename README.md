🚀 What is Web3?

Web3 is the next evolution of the internet where:

- Users own their data
- Money moves without intermediaries
- Applications run on blockchains, not centralized servers

In short:

«Web1 = Read
Web2 = Read + Write
Web3 = Read + Write + Own»

---

🔗 Core Concepts (Explained Simply)

🧱 Blockchain

A blockchain is a public, tamper-proof digital ledger that records transactions across many computers.

Examples:

- Bitcoin
- Ethereum
- Solana

---

👛 Wallets

A wallet lets you:

- Store crypto
- Sign transactions
- Prove ownership

Popular wallets:

- MetaMask
- Phantom
- Trust Wallet

«Not your keys, not your crypto.»

---

📜 Smart Contracts

Smart contracts are self-executing programs on the blockchain.

They:

- Run automatically
- Cannot be changed once deployed
- Remove middlemen

Used in:

- DeFi
- NFTs
- DAOs

---

🪙 Tokens

Tokens represent value or utility on a blockchain.

Types:

- Utility tokens
- Governance tokens
- Stablecoins
- NFTs

---

🖼 NFTs

NFTs are unique digital assets.

Used for:

- Art
- Gaming assets
- Identity
- Memberships

---

🏦 DeFi (Decentralized Finance)

DeFi replaces banks with code.

You can:

- Lend
- Borrow
- Trade
- Earn yield

All without permission.

---

🏛 DAOs

DAOs are internet-native organizations governed by token holders instead of CEOs.

Examples:

- MakerDAO
- Arbitrum DAO

---

🤖 Web3 + AI (Why It Matters)

AI helps Web3 by:

- Detecting fraud
- Analyzing on-chain data
- Powering trading bots
- Automating DAOs

Web3 helps AI by:

- Providing open data
- Removing centralized control

Together, they are powerful.
